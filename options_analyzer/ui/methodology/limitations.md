## Known limitations

* Constant volatility per leg (no smile dynamics in strategy valuation).
* European exercise for strategies.
* No bid-ask spreads or transaction costs beyond a flat per-contract commission.
* No margin or discrete dividends.
* Log-normal probabilities.
* Whole-day expiries of at least one day (no same-day options).

This tool is for education and analysis only and is not investment advice.
